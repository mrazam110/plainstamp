Plainstamp

About Plainstamp

Plainstamp explains German bureaucracy in plain words to people who don't read German well. You ask a question in your own language and get an answer with a link to the official page it came from.

How it works

  • We collect public pages from official German sources and split them into short passages.
  • When you ask, the system finds the passages that fit your question and writes the answer from those passages only.
  • Every answer shows its sources and the date we last checked each page. If we have nothing relevant, it says so and points you to the responsible office.

Coverage

The design covers all of Germany. For now we cover federal rules plus Berlin, Brandenburg and Bavaria. The other states are not covered yet, and the app tells you when that is the case. See "Coverage" in the menu.

Open source

Plainstamp is free and open-source software under the AGPL-3.0 licence. It is non-commercial: no ads, no accounts, no selling of data. Anyone can read the code, run their own copy, or improve it. If you run a modified version as a service, the AGPL asks you to share your changes under the same licence.

  • Region data is configuration. States, topics and sources live in plain YAML files, so adding a state means adding files, not changing code.
  • The AI model is swappable. The code talks to models through one interface, chosen by a setting.
  • Ways to help: add or check sources for a state, write test questions with verified answers, fix wrong or outdated answers, translate the interface, or improve the code.

Not an official service

Plainstamp is an independent project. It is not run by, endorsed by or connected to any German authority, and it does not use official logos. It gives general information, not legal advice. Rules and offices can differ by state and city, so check with the office before you act.

Privacy

The server does not keep your questions, answers or IP address. Your chat stays in your browser. Your question is sent to a third-party AI service to write the answer, so please don't type names, addresses or ID numbers. Details are on the privacy page.

Found a mistake?

Official pages change, and an answer can be out of date or wrong. Check the source link and the date shown with the answer. Reports and corrections are welcome through the project's issue tracker once the repository is public.
